# Wood background source

`wood.png` is the master texture generated with the built-in image_gen tool.
The asset generator resizes it to the game canvas and adds existing UI rules and footer text.
Tile faces retain their yellow, mint, and ivory palette.

Generation prompt:
A full-bleed warm medium-dark walnut wooden tabletop texture, flat directly overhead orthographic view, vertical fine wood grain, smooth matte finish, subtle natural tonal variations, warm brown amber undertones. Uniform soft lighting, quiet low contrast suitable behind cream text and colorful tiles. No edges, perspective, objects, writing, logos, borders, vignette, dramatic knots, or cracks. Portrait aspect ratio approximately 1:2.2.
